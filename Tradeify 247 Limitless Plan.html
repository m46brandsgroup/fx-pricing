    The A-book fund

    Every payout feeds a fund held for the trader. The fund comes out of the firm's share, so the trader still gets their full cash split. The fund becomes the first-loss capital on real, A-booked accounts. The firm's live risk is capped at what it has already set aside.

    1. Fund builds with each payoutOn every gross payout, 10% from the firm's share goes into the trader's fund. The trader still receives their full 75% in cash.
    2. Unlock at $10KWhen the fund hits $10,000, the trader unlocks live accounts. At a 10% fund rate, that means $100,000 of gross payouts.
    3. Fresh live accountsThe fund becomes a 20% static drawdown. Live allocation equals fund ÷ 20%. The trader can spread it across up to 4 accounts.
    4. Live split 90/10Profits on live accounts are split 90% to the trader and 10% to the firm. Positions are real and A-booked.
    5. Lose the fund, cool downIf live losses use up the fund, the trader goes on a 4-week cooldown before they can re-enter the live programme.
